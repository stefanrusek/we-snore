For a fair trial

  • Plug the phone in and leave this page open. It keeps the screen awake and dims itself.
  • Put it on a nightstand, closer to one sleeper than the other. Loudness is a clue.
  • In the morning, listen to each snorer's samples and put a name on them. We Snore remembers their snoreprint for next time.
  • Audio never leaves this device.

Awards ceremony

Snorers are told apart by the spectral shape, pitch and loudness of each snore, then grouped. Two people snoring in the very same breath get credited to the louder one. Loudness is relative to the room's quiet, not calibrated dB. This is for settling arguments, not diagnosing sleep apnea.
